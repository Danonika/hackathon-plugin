- [x] **C1** (critical/bug) `internal/evaluation/evaluation.go:682` - `ApplyCommission` unconditionally appends the resolved tie group to the *end* of `rest`, after every already-resolved entry. But `Rank` can leave an unresolved tie anywhere in the order (a tie group is contiguous, not necessarily last). Example: A=90, B=85, C=85, D=80 -> `Unresolved=[B,C]`, `rest=[A,D]`, and after the commission the result becomes `[A,D,B,C]`, ranking D (80) above the tied 85s.
